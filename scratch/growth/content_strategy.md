# Fast Food Guides - Content Strategy to 50k Sessions

## Current State & Goal
- **Current:** ~12k monthly sessions / 130 articles (~92 sessions/article/mo)
- **Target:** 50k monthly sessions
- **Gap:** Need to generate ~38k additional monthly sessions. Assuming conservative traffic scaling, we need to publish **150-200 new high-quality articles**.

## Strategic Approach
To scale rapidly without cannibalizing our current audience, we will expand horizontally into **new QSR chains** and vertically into **management and supervisory roles**. The management tier is severely underserved by generic food blogs and perfectly fits the "Russell Roseberry" persona.

### Topical Map Expansion

#### Pillar 1: Shift Supervisor & Management Operations
Focuses on the business side of the line. High-value search intent from newly promoted managers.
*   **Inventory Management:** COGS tracking, FIFO auditing, nightly counts, minimizing waste, variance reporting.
*   **Labor Management:** Optimizing the floor plan, cutting labor during slow periods, compliance with break laws, writing schedules.
*   **Team & Floor Ops:** De-escalating customer conflicts, managing call-outs, training new hires on the line, passing health inspections.

#### Pillar 2: New Chain Expansion
Expanding beyond our current chains to the highest-volume QSRs.
*   **Taco Bell:** Hot-holding safety, line assembly speed (Make-Line ops), inventory management for high-turnover ingredients.
*   **Chick-fil-A:** Drive-thru throughput tactics, iPad line-busting, breading station workflows.
*   **Wendy's:** Fresh beef grill operations, chili prep lifecycle, fry station timing.

#### Pillar 3: Equipment Troubleshooting & Maintenance
Highly specific, low-competition keywords searched by desperate night-shift workers.
*   Fryer vat boil-out procedures step-by-step.
*   Taylor shake/ice cream machine daily cleaning cycles.
*   Standard POS system crash recoveries and end-of-day register reconciliation.

## 6-Month Publishing Schedule
**Pace:** 30 articles per month (~1 per day).

*   **Month 1: Supervisor Basics (Labor & Floor Ops)**
    *   15 articles on shift management, break rotations, and conflict resolution.
    *   15 articles on POS troubleshooting and cash handling.
*   **Month 2: Inventory & Cost Control**
    *   20 articles focused purely on food cost, COGS, waste tracking, and FIFO.
    *   10 articles on equipment maintenance (fryers, grills).
*   **Month 3: Taco Bell Operations**
    *   30 articles covering stations, prep, and speed-of-service metrics.
*   **Month 4: Wendy's Operations**
    *   30 articles covering grill ops, food safety, and shift transitions.
*   **Month 5: Chick-fil-A Operations**
    *   30 articles covering drive-thru efficiency, breading, and expediting.
*   **Month 6: Advanced Management & Cross-Training**
    *   30 articles combining chain-specific management (e.g., "Taco Bell Inventory Variance", "Wendy's Labor Matrices").

## Writer Guidelines & Rule Enforcement
To prevent Google AdSense rejection for "Thin Content" and maintain the gritty 10-year kitchen manager persona of Russell Roseberry, all new content **MUST** adhere to the site's strict `.agents/AGENTS.md` rules:

1. **800-Word Minimum:** Deep dive into operational realities, no surface-level fluff.
2. **Zero Banned Phrases:** Strictly prohibit words like "Tapestry", "Crucial", "Delve into", "Furthermore". Use authentic, direct kitchen language.
3. **Mandatory Frontmatter & Schema:** Every article must have a `faq` array with exactly 2 operational questions (for JSON-LD).
4. **ProTips:** Minimum 2-3 `<div class="callout callout-tip">...</div>` or `<ProTip>` blocks per article sharing insider knowledge.
5. **Imagery:** At least one in-body markdown image demonstrating the process/equipment.
