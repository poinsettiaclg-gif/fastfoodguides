## Article Outline & Concept

**The Setup (The Nightmare):**
The reality of the line is brutal when your strongest fry cook ghosts you at 5:00 PM on a Friday. Tickets are already printing to the floor, the expo screen is red, and your remaining crew is looking at you to see if the ship is going down. 

**The Gritty Reality (Operations):**
Step by step, this is the workflow when you're down a man:
1. **Triaging the Stations:** Which stations can be consolidated immediately? We cover cross-training essentials.
2. **The 30-Second Shift:** Reallocating the strongest cross-trained staff to the bottleneck without breaking the line's rhythm.
3. **Menu Management:** Temporarily 86ing high-labor prep items or throttling digital orders if the POS allows it to buy the kitchen five minutes of breathing room.

**The Fix (The Affiliate Tie-in):**
What actually happens in most joints is a frantic group text trying to find coverage, which takes you off the floor when you're needed most. This is where 7shifts steps in. Instead of manual panic, you broadcast a shift-pool alert to all available staff across locations instantly. We highlight how the shift-bidding feature turns a disaster into a quick fix without the manager having to make twenty phone calls.
