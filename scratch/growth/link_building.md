# Fast Food Guides - Link Building Strategy

**Goal:** Increase Domain Authority to reach 50k sessions via unconventional PR and outreach, leveraging Russell Roseberry's 10-year QSR kitchen manager persona.

## Strategy 1: The "Viral Trend Reality Check" (Targeting Food & QSR Industry Pubs)
**The Concept:** When a fast food prep video goes viral on TikTok or a chain announces a major operational change (e.g., new automated grills), we publish a gritty, highly detailed operational teardown of *why* it works or why it's a disaster waiting to happen. 
**The Pitch:** Reach out to writers at QSR Magazine, Restaurant Business, Eater, and mainstream food blogs offering an "Ex-Kitchen Manager's Brutal Reality Check" on the trend.
**The Link:** They link back to our in-depth workflow guides or chain-specific operational breakdowns as the authoritative source on the reality of the line.

## Strategy 2: "The Hidden Economics of QSR Management" (Targeting Career & Business Sites)
**The Concept:** Develop a data-driven report exposing the reality of QSR management—detailing metrics outsiders miss like shrink penalties, actual bonus structures versus base pay, and the physical toll of 60-hour weeks on the line. 
**The Pitch:** Pitch this to career resources (The Muse, Glassdoor blog, Business Insider's career section) and labor journalists as an insider perspective on the "entry-level management trap."
**The Link:** High-DR career and business domains link to our career trajectory pages or management survival guides as recommended reading for prospective food service workers.

## Strategy 3: "Operational Menu Engineering" (Targeting Culinary & Food Science Pubs)
**The Concept:** Instead of basic "secret menu" hacks, we create deep-dive content on *how* fast food chains engineer ingredients for maximum cross-utilization (e.g., how one base sauce is chemically and operationally stretched across 10 menu items to reduce waste).
**The Pitch:** Pitch this to culinary schools, food science blogs, and major food publications (Serious Eats, The Takeout) as a masterclass in high-volume food waste reduction and kitchen efficiency.
**The Link:** These domains link back to our chain-specific ingredient and prep guides as reference material for culinary efficiency.
