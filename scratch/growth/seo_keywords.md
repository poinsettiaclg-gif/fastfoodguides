# Fast Food Guides: SEO Keyword Strategy for 50k Sessions

To push fastfoodguides.com from 12k to 50k monthly sessions, we need to lean hard into the actual pain points of shift managers, line cooks, and closing crews. We are targeting the stuff they frantically search for on their phones while hiding in the walk-in freezer. 

Here are 5 high-volume, low-competition content pillars built for the Russell Roseberry persona:

## Pillar 1: Equipment Troubleshooting (The "It's Broken Again" Cluster)
**Target Keywords:** Taylor ice cream machine error codes, reset Frymaster deep fryer, clamshell grill calibration, bypass fast food oven errors, shake machine lockout.
**Strategy:** Nobody calls the service tech until they've spent 20 minutes trying to fix it themselves. These are high-intent searches from stressed managers staring at a blinking red light on a Friday night. Step-by-step troubleshooting guides will dominate this niche.

## Pillar 2: Audit & Inspection Survival
**Target Keywords:** EcoSure audit checklist fast food, Steritech critical violations, passing QSR health inspection, fast food temp log cheat sheet, how to clean ice machine fast food.
**Strategy:** Corporate audits dictate manager bonuses and jobs. General managers and shift leads desperately look for checklists and common failure points before the auditor shows up. We can provide actionable checklists and brutal truths about what auditors actually check (like the gaskets on the prep table).

## Pillar 3: Drive-Thru Speed & Metric Hacks
**Target Keywords:** SOS timer hacks fast food, lowering drive-thru window times, fast food order sequencing, drive-thru bottleneck solutions, how to clear a drive-thru rush.
**Strategy:** Drive-thru times (Speed of Service) are the most heavily tracked metric in the industry. Shift runners are constantly searching for ways to shave seconds off the timer. Articles detailing how to stage orders, manage the expo screen, and keep the line moving will generate heavy repeat traffic.

## Pillar 4: Inventory & Variance Control
**Target Keywords:** fast food inventory count sheet, reducing food waste QSR, fixing negative variance fast food, yield troubleshooting fast food, missing inventory fast food.
**Strategy:** A bad inventory count ruins food cost numbers and gets people written up. Content explaining how to track down missing product—like checking the grease trap, reviewing waste buckets, or fixing cashier ring-errors—provides massive value to newly promoted managers.

## Pillar 5: De-Escalation & Front Counter Nightmares
**Target Keywords:** handling angry fast food customers, fast food refund policy scams, de-escalating drive-thru fights, fake hundred dollar bill fast food, spotting counterfeit money QSR.
**Strategy:** Burnout usually comes from dealing with the general public. Practical, gritty advice on bouncing scammers, identifying fake bills, and handling screaming customers without getting fired gets shared heavily among industry workers (and does great on Reddit).
