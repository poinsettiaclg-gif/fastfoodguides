# Pitch: The 5-Minute Death Spiral: Why EcoSure is Failing Your Store

**Author:** Russell Roseberry
**Category:** Store Operations / Audits

**Concept:**
An anxiety-inducing breakdown of what actually happens when an unannounced Steritech or EcoSure auditor walks through the doors, focusing on the microscopic operational failures that tank a store's score in the first five minutes.

**Outline & Gritty Details:**
- **The Initial Hit:** The reality of the line is that the moment the auditor pulls out their thermometer, the shift changes. We cover the immediate triage steps managers must take.
- **The Quat Trap:** What actually happens with the sanitizer buckets. The dispenser is slightly off, the rag absorbs the active ingredient, and suddenly your quat PPM is reading at 100 instead of 200. Boom, critical violation.
- **The Hold Timer Graveyard:** Prep stations filled with sliced tomatoes or shredded lettuce where the hold timers expired 20 minutes ago because the lunch rush hit hard. 
- **Pencil-Whipped Temp Logs:** The contrast between the perfectly filled-out temp book and the actual temperature of the walk-in cooler holding the raw chicken.
- **Survival Workflow:** Step by step, this is the workflow to get the crew tightening up the line while the manager stalls the auditor in the lobby.

**Why it works for the site:**
Hits the nerve of every QSR manager. It's high-stakes, realistic, and packed with insider operational knowledge that corporate manuals ignore, strictly adhering to the requested gritty and authentic tone.
