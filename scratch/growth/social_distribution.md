# Organic Social Distribution Strategy for fastfoodguides.com

## Goal: 12k -> 50k Monthly Sessions
## Core Philosophy: Value-First, Insider-Led Distribution

As "Russell Roseberry", a gritty, 10-year QSR kitchen manager, the key to surviving Reddit and TikTok is extreme authenticity. No polished marketing speak, no AI-sounding buzzwords (following our writing rules), just the reality of the line.

### 1. Reddit Strategy: The "Trojan Horse" AMA and Deep Dives
Reddit communities like `r/fastfood`, `r/KitchenConfidential`, and brand-specific subs (`r/McDonaldsEmployees`, `r/tacobell`) aggressively ban marketers. We win by being an insider first.

**Tactic A: The "War Stories" Post**
- **Action**: Post highly detailed, text-heavy stories about specific operational nightmares or secrets in `r/KitchenConfidential` or `r/fastfood`. (e.g., "Why the ice cream machine is actually broken: A 10-year manager's explanation.")
- **Link Placement**: Do not link in the post body. Let the post gain traction organically. If users ask for more details or "how do you know this?", reply in the comments with: "I've been writing a lot of these operational breakdowns down here [link to specific article]."
- **Frequency**: 1 post per week, rotating subreddits.

**Tactic B: The Helpful Veteran in Employee Subs**
- **Action**: Monitor `r/McDonaldsEmployees`, `r/Chipotle`, etc., for posts from new trainees asking for help (e.g., "How do I close the prep station?").
- **Link Placement**: Reply with a highly detailed, accurate answer in the comment. At the end, add: "I wrote a whole SOP on this over here if you need to pull it up on your phone during your shift: [link]."
- **Why it works**: It’s contextual, helpful, and drives highly intent-driven users who will bookmark the site.

### 2. TikTok Strategy: The Faceless "POV: Kitchen Manager"
TikTok favors raw, unpolished, insider info. 

**Tactic A: "What Corporate Doesn't Want You to Know" Series**
- **Format**: Faceless videos. B-roll of generic kitchen prep or line work (or stock video styled to look like a phone recording), with a text overlay and gritty voiceover (use a tired, raspy TTS or real voice).
- **Content**: Break down one specific fact from an article. E.g., "The real reason your fries are soggy at 2 PM." 
- **Call to Action**: "Full breakdown of how the fry station actually works is in the link in my bio." (Link to the specific article).

**Tactic B: "Trainee Survival Guides"**
- **Format**: Rapid-fire tips for new QSR workers. "3 things every new line cook gets wrong."
- **Call to Action**: "If you're training, I built a free cheat sheet site for you. Link in bio."

### 3. Avoiding the Spam Filter
- **The 90/10 Rule on Reddit**: For every 1 comment that drops a link, leave 9 comments that are purely helpful or participating in the community banter.
- **Never use UTM parameters on Reddit**: Reddit users spot tracking links instantly and will report them. Use clean links.
- **TikTok Link-in-Bio**: Use a simple Linktree or direct link to the site's most popular hub page.
