# B2B Growth Strategy: Attracting Restaurant Operators & GMs

## Objective
Capture a 50k audience of high-value QSR Operators and General Managers to drive B2B affiliate conversions for partners like Toast POS and 7shifts.

## Core Angle
Leverage the gritty, authentic "Russell Roseberry" persona. GMs ignore corporate fluff. They want actionable, battle-tested operational tactics from someone who has actually run the line and managed prime costs.

## High-Converting Article Concepts
1. **"Stop Bleeding Labor: Fixing the Mid-Shift Transition"**
   - **Focus:** Managing overlapping labor during shift changes without blowing up prime costs.
   - **Affiliate Tie-in:** 7shifts (demonstrating how smart scheduling prevents overlap waste).
2. **"Why Your POS is Slowing Down Your Drive-Thru (And Costing You Cars)"**
   - **Focus:** System lag, clunky modifier screens, and order routing bottlenecks.
   - **Affiliate Tie-in:** Toast POS (speed of service, kitchen display system integration).
3. **"The Sunday Night Inventory Count: Tracking the Missing Cheese"**
   - **Focus:** Real-world tactics for tracking variance and shrinkage on high-cost items.
   - **Affiliate Tie-in:** Toast POS (automated inventory management features).

## Lead-Magnet Tools (For Email Capture & Affiliate CTAs)
1. **Dynamic Prime Cost Calculator (Web Tool or Google Sheet):**
   - GMs input weekly sales, COGS, and labor. The tool flags if they are over the standard 60% mark.
   - *CTA:* "Get labor under control with 7shifts."
2. **Shift Readiness & Line Station Checklist:**
   - Downloadable PDF/Spreadsheet for shift managers to verify prep levels.
   - *CTA:* "Move checklists to the cloud with Toast/7shifts."
3. **Overtime Assassin Tracker:**
   - A schedule variance tool tracking scheduled vs. actual hours to kill overtime before it happens.

## Distribution & SEO
- SEO targeting long-tail operational queries (e.g., "reduce QSR turnover", "QSR prime cost industry standard", "manage fast food labor cost").
- Organic social distribution on LinkedIn, targeting users with titles like "General Manager", "Area Director", or "Franchisee" at major chains.
