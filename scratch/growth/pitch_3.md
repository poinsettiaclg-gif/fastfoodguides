# Pitch: Beating the Taylor C602: A Shift Manager's Survival Guide to the "Heat Cycle" Death Loop

**Target Audience:** Shift managers, QSR operators
**Estimated Length:** 1,200 words
**SEO Keywords:** Taylor C602 error codes, McDonald's ice cream machine broken, C602 heat cycle fault, reset Taylor soft serve machine

## Angle & Rationale
Every shift manager has faced the Friday night nightmare: the Taylor C602 soft-serve machine locking out with a cryptic error. The reality of the line is that the machine isn't mechanically broken 90% of the time—it's soft-locked by a failed 14-day heat cycle or an overfill issue. This article strips away the technician jargon and gives a gritty, step-by-step workflow for managers to decode the lockout screens, clear the false faults, and survive the rush without a $300 service call. 

## Proposed Outline
1. **The Myth of the Broken Machine:** What actually happens when the C602 goes down (sensor trips vs. actual mechanical failure).
2. **Decoding the Lockout Screen:** How to navigate past the generic maintenance warning to read the actual error codes (e.g., `ERROR 04: HEAT CYCLE`).
3. **The Hopper Overfill Trap:** Explaining the physics of how a rushing closer overfilling the hopper triggers the pasteurization failure at 2 AM.
4. **The Manager's Emergency Reset:** Step by step, this is the workflow to clean the sensor prongs, clear the fault, and get the machine back online without violating health codes.
5. **Preventative Tactics:** Routine lubrication and sensor wiping to ensure the machine survives the weekend rush.
