## The Angle
For decades, the rumor mill has churned out claims that Taco Bell uses "Grade D" meat or uses sand as a filler. Russell tackles this head-on. The reality of the line is less sensational but physically harsher: the beef is 88% USDA-inspected beef and 12% filler (oats, spices, water to hold texture). The real story isn't a secret grade of meat, but the brutal, daily operational hazard of the "rethermalizer"—fishing scalding hot, 5-pound plastic bags of precooked beef out of boiling water during a massive rush without burning your arms off.

## Required FAQ Schema Candidates
- *Question:* Does Taco Bell use Grade D meat in their tacos?
  *Answer:* No. The beef is standard USDA-inspected beef mixed with a 12% blend of oats, seasonings, and water to maintain moisture and texture on the hot line.
- *Question:* How is Taco Bell beef cooked inside the restaurant?
  *Answer:* The seasoned beef arrives precooked in sealed plastic bags. We drop those bags into a rethermalizer (a commercial boiling water bath) for 45 minutes until they hit the required safe holding temperature, then slice them open to pan the meat.
