# Content Cannibalization Audit: 6-Month Expansion Plan

**Verdict:** Highly Risky. The proposed velocity is a fast track to keyword cannibalization and SERP ranking dilution.

## The Cannibalization Threat
Publishing 150-200 new articles in 6 months—essentially doubling the site's footprint—poses a massive risk to the existing 130 articles. 
- **Topic Overlap:** Writing 30+ articles on "management," "inventory," or "floor ops" (Months 1, 2, and 6) will inevitably overlap with our existing operational guides. A new post on "Taco Bell Inventory Variance" could easily cannibalize an existing guide on "Taco Bell Prep and Food Waste."
- **Search Intent Dilution:** When multiple pages on our site answer the same user query (e.g., "how to manage fast food food cost"), Google will struggle to determine which page to rank, resulting in neither page ranking well.
- **Velocity vs. Quality:** Pushing 30 articles a month guarantees topic bleed. Writers (or agents) will inevitably re-tread existing ground to hit word count minimums (800+ words).

## How to Avoid Cannibalization

1. **Content Mapping & Gap Analysis:** Before writing a single new word, map the existing 130 articles to their primary target keywords. Only approve new topics that have zero search intent overlap with current pages.
2. **Hub-and-Spoke Architecture:** Clearly separate "Basic Ops" from "Management Ops." If we have an existing article on "Wendy's Grill Operations," don't write a new one for managers. Instead, create a distinct "Wendy's Kitchen Shift Lead Guide" (Hub) and link out to the existing grill operations guide (Spoke), updating the spoke to include management tips.
3. **Prune and Upgrade First:** Instead of blindly creating 150 new pages, consolidate. If an existing article covers inventory loosely, update it rather than publishing a separate "Advanced Inventory" post. 
4. **Strict URL and Keyword Scoping:** Assign exact long-tail keywords (e.g., "Taylor shake machine daily cleaning cycle C602") to ensure we aren't targeting generic terms like "fast food shake machine cleaning" which might already be ranking.
