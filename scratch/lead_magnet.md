# THE 5-MINUTE AUDIT SURVIVAL CHECKLIST

**Print this out. Tape it to the manager's station. Do not fail an audit because of something stupid.** 
Auditors (EcoSure, Steritech, Board of Health) decide whether you pass or fail in the first five minutes they walk through the door. 

If they see these 5 things handled perfectly, they will relax. If they see even one of these missed, they will rip your store apart.

## 1. THE SINK (The 3-Compartment Lifeline)
The absolute first place they look is the 3-compartment sink. 
* **[ ] SANITIZER BUCKETS:** Are there fresh sanitizer buckets on the floor/counters? Grab a test strip IMMEDIATELY when they walk in. If it’s not reading 200 PPM (Quat), dump and remake it instantly.
* **[ ] HANDWASHING SINKS:** Are they completely clear? If a line cook threw a dirty spatula in the hand sink, you automatically lose 5 points. Get it out. Ensure paper towels and soap are fully stocked. 
* **[ ] THE TEMP LOG:** Is your daily line temp log sitting on the clipboard, fully filled out? If it’s blank, you’re dead.

## 2. THE WALK-IN COOLER (The Trap)
They will walk in here within the first 3 minutes.
* **[ ] LABELS AND DATES:** Scan the entire walk-in. If there is a single pan of prepped tomatoes or sliced onions without a day dot/label, you lose points.
* **[ ] THE FLOOR:** Are there boxes on the floor? Everything must be 6 inches off the ground.
* **[ ] RAW MEAT HIERARCHY:** Raw chicken on the absolute bottom shelf. Raw beef above it. Ready-to-eat (cheese, lettuce) on the top shelf. Never let raw meat drip on anything else.

## 3. THE PREP LINE (The Warzone)
* **[ ] TEMPERATURE DANGER ZONE:** Grab your calibrated thermometer and temp the most vulnerable item on the line (usually sliced tomatoes or shredded cheese). It must be below 41°F. If it's 43°F, throw it in the walk-in immediately and tell the auditor "We just swapped these from the cooler."
* **[ ] TONGS AND SPATULAS:** If a pair of tongs fell on the floor, is a line cook still using it? Stop them immediately. 
* **[ ] ICE MACHINE:** Are the scoops stored *outside* the ice machine in a clean, sanitized holder? Never leave the scoop inside the ice bin.

## 4. THE TEAM (The Weakest Link)
* **[ ] HATS AND HAIRNETS:** Is everyone wearing a hat or hairnet? 
* **[ ] DRINKS:** Are there unauthorized employee drinks on the prep line? All drinks must have a lid and a straw, and must be stored on a bottom shelf AWAY from food.
* **[ ] GLOVES:** The moment the auditor walks in, scream "Wash hands and change gloves!" to the entire line. The auditor will note that you enforce handwashing.

## 5. THE PAPERWORK
* **[ ] SERVSAFE CERTIFICATE:** Do you know exactly where the Manager's ServSafe/Food Handler certificate is posted?
* **[ ] PEST CONTROL LOG:** Do you have the binder from Ecolab/Orkin showing your last inspection?
