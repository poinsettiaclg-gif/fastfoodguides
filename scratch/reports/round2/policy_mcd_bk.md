# AdSense Policy Audit: McDonald's & Burger King Articles

## Overview
An audit was conducted on 15 articles related to McDonald's and Burger King in the `src/content/articles` directory. The review checked for potential Google AdSense policy violations, specifically focusing on:
- Dangerous content
- Explicit material
- Copyright infringement
- Shocking content

## Findings
**Overall Status:** **PASSED** (No AdSense policy violations found).

### 1. Shocking / Dangerous Content
- **Workplace Hazards (No Violation):** Several articles accurately describe kitchen hazards. For example, `burger-king-broiler-closing.md` mentions a "dangerous steam explosion" and "third-degree burn," while `mcdonalds-q-ing-oven.md` mentions a "severe burn hazard." These are presented in a purely educational, workplace-safety context. They do not promote dangerous acts, nor do they contain gratuitously graphic or shocking descriptions that would trigger AdSense filters.
- **Food Processing (No Violation):** `mcdonalds-nugget-process.md` debunks the "pink slime" myth and details factory meat processing. The content is written objectively, focusing on food science, and avoids any gruesome or shocking language.

### 2. Explicit Content
- **No Violation:** All articles maintain a professional, documentary-style tone focused on QSR (Quick Service Restaurant) kitchen operations, equipment maintenance, and employee training. No sexually explicit, suggestive, or profanity-laced content was found.

### 3. Copyright / Intellectual Property
- **Fair Use (No Violation):** The articles discuss corporate processes and proprietary equipment (such as the Taylor C709 soft-serve machine or Nieco broilers) as well as public controversies like the Kytch diagnostic tool lawsuit (`mcdonalds-ice-cream-machine-truth.md`). This constitutes fair-use educational commentary and journalism. No unauthorized copyrighted media or illegal materials are shared.

## Conclusion
The McDonald's and Burger King articles are fully compliant with Google AdSense publisher policies. The content is educational, safe for all audiences, and cleared for monetization.
