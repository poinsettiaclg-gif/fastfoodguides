# EEAT Trust & Transparency Audit Report

**Date:** July 31, 2026
**Target Pages:** `contact.astro`, `privacy.astro`, `terms.astro`, `disclaimer.astro`

## Executive Summary
An audit of the core policy and contact pages indicates exceptionally strong Trust and Transparency signals aligned with Google’s E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness) guidelines. The site clearly defines its operational entity, establishes strict editorial boundaries, and provides comprehensive disclaimers that protect users while establishing the site as an independent, professional resource.

## Detailed Findings

### 1. Contact Page (`contact.astro`)
* **Transparency of Ownership:** Prominently features the physical business address for "Fast Food Guides Media LLC" in Carson City, NV, establishing real-world legitimacy.
* **Accessibility:** Offers both a direct email (`contact@fastfoodguides.com`) and a categorized contact form.
* **Commitment to Accuracy:** Specifically includes a "Corrections & Updates" category in the contact form, signaling to users and search engines that the publication is committed to maintaining accurate, up-to-date information.
* **Structured Data:** Implements `ContactPage` and `Organization` schema markup, helping search engines confidently associate the brand with its contact details.

### 2. Privacy Policy (`privacy.astro`)
* **Regulatory Compliance:** Includes dedicated sections for GDPR (European Economic Area) and CCPA (California Residents), demonstrating a commitment to global privacy standards.
* **Clarity of Data Usage:** Clearly explains the use of Google Analytics and AdSense, and provides direct links for users to opt-out.
* **Consistency:** Repeats the physical entity address at the bottom of the page, ensuring consistent business NAP (Name, Address, Phone/Contact) signals across the site.

### 3. Terms of Service (`terms.astro`)
* **Boundary Setting:** Explicitly states that content is for "informational and educational purposes only" and is not a substitute for official corporate training.
* **Editorial Independence:** The "No Affiliation" section clearly separates the site from the fast-food brands it covers, establishing it as an independent commentary and research archive.
* **Food Safety Liability:** Sets clear expectations that users must follow local health regulations and ServSafe guidelines rather than relying solely on the site, demonstrating responsible publishing.

### 4. Disclaimer Page (`disclaimer.astro`)
* **Comprehensive Risk Mitigation:** Covers a wide spectrum of potential liabilities, including Food Safety, Medical/Allergies, Legal/Insurance, and Financial/Tax. 
* **User Safety First:** Explicitly warns users about cross-contamination and severe allergies, advising them to consult physicians or official restaurant allergen menus.
* **Advertising Transparency:** Openly discloses the use of advertising networks like Google AdSense while reaffirming that "editorial content is independent and not influenced by advertisers."

## Conclusion
The audited pages provide excellent trust signals. The presence of a verifiable business entity, physical address, and detailed, sector-specific disclaimers (especially regarding food safety and allergens) strongly supports the site's authority and trustworthiness in the YMYL (Your Money or Your Life) aspects of food service and employment. No immediate changes are required.
