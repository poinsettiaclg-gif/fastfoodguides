# EEAT & Trust Assessment

Overall, the core pages (`about.astro`, `contact.astro`, `privacy.astro`, `terms.astro`, and `disclaimer.astro`) successfully establish strong trust, professionalism, and satisfy Google's EEAT (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) criteria.

## 1. About Page (`about.astro`)
- **Experience & Expertise:** Prominently features Russell Roseberry’s 10-year QSR and Culinary background (from crew member to multi-unit kitchen manager for Wendy's, Domino's, Panera). 
- **Trustworthiness:** Establishes strict editorial standards (articles are written from direct experience, verified against corporate standards, and independent).
- **Authoritativeness:** Includes proper `AboutPage` schema markup and explicitly names the publishing entity, Fast Food Guides Media LLC.

## 2. Contact Page (`contact.astro`)
- **Professionalism:** Features a clean, structured form with specific inquiry categories (e.g., Corrections, Business Inquiries, Contributors).
- **Transparency:** Clearly lists a physical business address (Carson City, NV) and a professional email, alongside response time expectations (1-2 business days), which significantly boosts trustworthiness.

## 3. Privacy Policy (`privacy.astro`)
- **Compliance:** Thoroughly outlines data collection practices (Google Analytics, AdSense), cookie usage, and data retention.
- **User Rights:** Specifically addresses regulatory frameworks like GDPR (for EEA residents) and CCPA (for California residents), as well as Children's Privacy (COPPA), demonstrating high legal professionalism.

## 4. Terms of Service (`terms.astro`)
- **Clarity & Safety:** Includes critical disclaimers emphasizing that content is for educational purposes and not a substitute for official corporate training. 
- **Liability Protection:** Prominently features a Food Safety Disclaimer referencing ServSafe and local health codes, which is crucial for YMYL (Your Money or Your Life) content. Defines user conduct and limitation of liability.

## 5. Disclaimer (`disclaimer.astro`)
- **Risk Mitigation:** Explicitly disclaims offering medical/allergy, legal/insurance, or financial/tax advice. 
- **Transparency:** Clearly outlines the advertising model (Google AdSense) while reiterating editorial independence and no corporate affiliations.

**Conclusion:** The pages robustly satisfy EEAT standards by detailing deep first-hand experience, transparently sharing publisher contact and entity information, and protecting users (and the site) through comprehensive safety and legal disclaimers.
